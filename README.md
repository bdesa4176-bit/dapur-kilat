Dapur Kilat (Warung Rush) — a fast little cooking game: take orders from the board, fetch ingredients from the counters, chop on the board, fry on the stove, plate the dish and serve it at the window before the timer runs out. Three recipes (Nasi Goreng, Mie Ayam, Soto Ayam) mix six ingredients in different ways, orders pile up as your level rises, and the shift ends when the clock runs out or three orders walk out the door.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD / arrows to move, E / Space to pick up, chop (hold), fry, plate and serve. Click works too; P pauses, M mutes.
